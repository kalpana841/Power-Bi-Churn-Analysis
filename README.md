Developed an end-to-end **Customer Churn Analysis** project to identify customers who are likely to leave a company and understand the key factors responsible for customer churn. The main objective of this project was to analyze customer behavior, identify high-risk customer segments, predict potential churn, and generate useful business insights that can help organizations improve customer retention and reduce revenue loss.

The project used **SQL, Python, Machine Learning, and Power BI** to complete the entire data analysis workflow. The customer dataset was first cleaned and prepared for analysis by handling missing values, duplicate records, inconsistent data, and incorrect data formats. Data preprocessing helped ensure that the dataset was accurate, consistent, and suitable for further analysis.

**SQL** was used for data extraction, transformation, and analysis. Different SQL queries were created to analyze customer information, subscription details, contract types, payment methods, customer tenure, monthly charges, and churn status. Filtering, grouping, aggregation, and other SQL operations were used to identify important patterns and understand the characteristics of customers who were more likely to leave the company.

**Python and Machine Learning** were used for exploratory data analysis and churn prediction. Python libraries such as **Pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib, and Seaborn** were used for data manipulation, analysis, and visualization. Exploratory Data Analysis helped identify relationships between different customer attributes and churn. Machine Learning techniques were then applied to historical customer data to predict potential customer churn and identify customers who may be at higher risk of leaving.

An interactive **Power BI dashboard** was developed to present the analysis and insights in a simple and user-friendly format. The dashboard included important metrics such as **total customers, churned customers, churn rate, customer segments, revenue, contract types, monthly charges, and key churn factors**. Interactive charts, graphs, slicers, and filters allowed users to explore customer behavior and compare different customer segments.

The project generated meaningful business insights that can help organizations understand customer behavior and the major factors affecting customer churn. These insights can support businesses in identifying high-risk customers and developing targeted customer retention strategies. Overall, the project demonstrates practical knowledge of **SQL, Python, Machine Learning, data cleaning, exploratory data analysis, Power BI, data visualization, and business intelligence**, while showing the ability to transform raw customer data into meaningful and actionable business insights.
